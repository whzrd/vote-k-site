About Kieran

Most people blame City Hall when things break. To fix it, we have to understand the machine. Council does not run the city day to day, their job is to steer it. They set the destination and protect the community.

Born and raised in Powell River, I have spent over 20 years leading large teams and building complex systems in the private sector. I will use my position to move the council from consultant-led governance to professional coordination. I know where the levers of power are, and I know how to pull them to get results.

ROOTED IN COMMUNITY: My family has helped shape this town for generations. From my grandparents, Finola (architect of Ocean View School and the museum) and Billy (a veteran), to my father Fred, a lifelong logger, and my mother Jayne, a dance instructor with the National School of Ballet and an avid gardener. With a brother who continues to shape our town's visual identity as a local muralist, my roots here are deep. I am committed to preserving that legacy by ensuring our city's future is viable.

The Mill Site

The mill site is a huge part of our future, but the City does not have enough power to protect it. I will take direct action on three fronts:

  • 1. REPEAL ARTICLE 21: I will ask the Province to repeal the rule that limits City jurisdiction over the mill site's environmental impacts. We must protect our land and water.
  • 2. DATA CENTER MORATORIUM: No new data centers until independent assessments on water, electricity, and neighborhoods are made public.
  • 3. PUBLIC CONTROL: I will use town halls and surveys to ensure residents, not outside interests, influence these decisions.

Core Priorities

The Flex-Hub

A consolidated facility for recreation, maker spaces, and emergency shelter. We will use lifecycle costing to avoid grant traps and keep our footprint small.

Permanent Housing

Move from emergency shelters to permanent homes. I support using city land for non-market housing and direct funding for co-ops and non-profits.

Local Economy

The city should prefer local providers and become a certified Living Wage Employer. I prioritize Social Return on Investment over expensive consultant reports.

Shared Resources

Moving beyond simple compromise to a Shared Resource approach with the Tla'amin Nation. This creates win-wins for all residents.

Vision for Youth

A livable city for young people. That means projects that actually get finished and systems that are coordinated and work together.

Climate and Water

Cut city emissions and safeguard our water quality. I support grants for local carbon-reduction projects in collaboration with the RD and Tla'amin Nation.

A Name Change Takes Generations of Understanding
"History is a line. A name existed here before Powell River, and Powell River remains the name for its specific era. Adding a new name doesn't erase the old one; it simply records the change and preserves the history of both."
